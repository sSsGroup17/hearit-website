Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

HearIt is a personal music player that lets users import supported audio files and enjoy their own music library. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed and handled when you use HearIt.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, HearIt may access audio files you select, music metadata such as song titles and artist names, and information needed to provide the app's features.

If you use Google services, HearIt may also receive information associated with your Google account and the permissions you authorize.

2. Music Files and URLs

You can import supported audio files and, where supported, provide audio URLs. The app uses these sources to provide music playback. How files, URLs, and related metadata are stored depends on the features you use and your app settings.

3. Google Services

If HearIt uses Google Sign-In or Google Drive integration, Google may share information with the app according to the permissions you grant. The app should access only the data necessary for the features you use and the permissions authorized by you.

Google's use of information is governed by Google's own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy .

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by HearIt may be used to provide music playback, organize your library, manage playlists and favorites, and operate any account or cloud features you use.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

HearIt's actual data-sharing practices depend on its implementation and the services it uses. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate enabled features or when disclosure is legally required. We do not promise that information is never shared; the app's actual practices must match this policy.

6. Storage and Security

Information may be stored on your device or by services used by enabled features. Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can choose which music to import and which optional integrations to use. You can manage Google account permissions through your Google Account settings. Removing a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

8. Children's Privacy

HearIt is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe personal information has been handled inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HearIt changes. The latest version will be published on this page, with its updated date.
